## What each delete removes (check 3, check 5)

All of it is found by id or foreign key. The new code reads no name, email or text pattern (check 1 tests the source).

**deleteStudent(studentId)**
- The child's sessions, with their notes and note skills, and Vienne's AI note drafts for those sessions.
- Their homework and answers (submissions), their redo list, and the assignments only they had.
- Their FAST results, progress reports with ratings, resource shares and subjects.
- The alerts linked to them (`Notification.studentId`).
- The stored files only their work used.
- On the code their parent redeemed (`Invite.usedById = parentId`), only the planned entry whose `studentId` is this child. No other code is touched.

**deleteFamily(parentId)**
- The parent's account, sign-in links (Account) and push devices.
- Every child, with everything listed above.
- The parent's conversation and messages.
- Their uploads and the stored files only they used.
- The invite code they redeemed (`usedById`).
- The alerts linked to them: their own (userId), plus `Notification.parentId` and the children's `studentId`.
- Their wrong-code rows. Their marks on others' rows stay without them, as in S23.
- Codes they never redeemed stay, including ones Vienne sent to their email. Alerts with no link stay.
