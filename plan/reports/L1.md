## /privacy, full text for Javaris to review

> **Privacy**
> What Mentoring Minds Academy keeps about your family, who sees it, and how to have it deleted.
>
> **What the app stores**
> - Your account: your name and email. If you sign in with Google, Google shares only those two.
> - Your children’s first names and grades.
> - Sessions, and Vienne’s notes about them.
> - Homework, and the photos of it you send.
> - Messages between you and Vienne.
> - FAST scores.
> - Invite requests: what you write when you ask for an invite.
>
> **Who sees it**
> Vienne sees everything, so she can teach. Each family sees only its own children and messages, never another family’s.
>
> **Where it lives**
> The app runs on Vercel, and the data is kept in a Neon database. Both are in the US.
>
> **How long it’s kept**
> Invite requests are deleted after 90 days. Everything else stays while your family uses the app.
>
> **How to delete it**
> Ask Vienne, and she deletes your family, or one child, with everything stored about them. Copies in the database’s backups clear on their own once its restore window passes.
